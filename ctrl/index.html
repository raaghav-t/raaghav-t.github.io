01 · Distributed-parameter systems · Apr-Jun 2026

Reduce the model.
Keep the behavior.

A boundary-actuated damped-wave PDE became a practical LQG study: discretize the field, shape disturbances, design output feedback, then ask how much controller order the full plant really needs.

  • Built a 99-state finite-difference plant with frequency-shaped disturbances and measurement noise.
  • Designed LQR and Kalman-filter controllers for three disturbance bandwidths.
  • Tested balanced-truncation orders 1-10 on the unreduced plant across damping regimes.

Transferable method: distributed-field modeling, reduction, disturbance estimation, and output feedback—the same toolkit used when state is spread across a thermal or energy system. This plant is a simulated linear fluid, not a thermal experiment.

02 · Constrained optimization · Jan-Mar 2026

Trade speed for effort—on purpose.

Three controllers, one six-state orbital model, and the same randomized starts: a direct comparison of what changes when limits and fuel priorities enter the objective.

  • Zero-order-hold discretized Hill-Clohessy-Wiltshire relative dynamics at a 10-second sample time.
  • Implemented saturated LQR, state-focused constrained MPC, and fuel-prioritized economic MPC.
  • Compared feasibility, convergence, time, and cumulative L1 input effort across 50 matched cases.

03 · Physical control · Apr-Jun 2025

Identify it. Balance it. Catch it.

A physical cart-pendulum closed the gap between model identification and real-time behavior through estimation, energy shaping, local optimal control, and explicit mode transitions.

Modeled + simulated course research

Hybrid Dynamics of DNA Supercoiling Regulation

Modeled continuous mechanical relaxation interrupted by threshold- and dwell-time-triggered enzyme jumps, making a biological homeostasis question explicit as a hybrid automaton.

  • Defined flow and jump maps, enzyme-reset timing, and a homeostatic target band.
  • Derived a candidate dwell-time bound and explored weak forward invariance.
  • Ran MATLAB Hybrid Systems Toolbox ensembles and enzyme-action ablations.

Scope: derived and numerically explored; this version does not claim global asymptotic stability or strong forward invariance.

Literature synthesis + reproduction / extension

Biological Self-Attention: Neuron-Astrocyte Networks

Mapped a published neuron-astrocyte circuit onto query, key, value, read/write, and normalization operations, then extended its ALBERT normalization comparison to biologically motivated widths.

  • Explained how alternating neural and astrocyte dynamics can approximate attention normalization.
  • Compared rodent- and human-scale influence widths with an ALBERT softmax trace over roughly 200 tokens.
  • Observed a qualitative trend toward closer tracking at the larger width.

Scope: exploratory reproduction of published Kozachkov et al. work — developed my own understanding of transformers.

Modeled + simulated course analysis

Robust Loop-Shaping for a Flexible Multimode Plant

Used zero-, one-, and ten-mode models to expose the cost of neglected resonances, then shaped complementary sensitivity against a multiplicative-uncertainty bound.

  • Built a 22nd-order “true” flexible plant from ten quadratic modal factors.
  • Showed an aggressive nominal controller destabilizing the ten-mode system.
  • Tuned a lower-gain design that remained bounded with closely matched one- and ten-mode step responses.

Scope: MATLAB simulation and loop-shaping—not implemented H∞ synthesis, certified robust performance, or hardware control.
