My work connects biological regulation, particle-laden flows, wind-tunnel experiments, and control-oriented modeling through a common question: what makes a physical system behave the way it does?

Research streams

Biological Control Lab

Control-oriented modeling of transcription and DNA supercoiling dynamics, including a co-authored manuscript.

M&M Flow Lab

Clogging dynamics in constricted, particle-laden flows with experiment automation and image-based event tracking.

Fluid Energy Science Lab

Wind-energy-inspired flow experiments and custom DAQ systems for wake characterization.

Throughline

Across cells, flows, and machines, I look for the state, constraint, or feedback pathway that explains observable behavior.

That perspective lets experiments inform models—and lets models suggest the next useful experiment.
